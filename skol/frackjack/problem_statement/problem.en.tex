\problemname{Frack Jack}
Alice and Bertil are throwing a ball in a park. Exactly $21$ meters in front of Alice and Bertil there is a post, and Jack gives Alice and Bertil a challenge.

The challenge is for Alice and Bertil to each throw a ball as close to the post as possible, but the ball may not be thrown past the post.
Whoever throws closest to $21$ meters, without throwing past $21$ meters, wins. If both Alice and Bertil throw farther than $21$ meters, or if they throw equally far, then Jack wins instead.
They have now thrown and need help determining who won.

\section*{Input}
The first line of input contains the integer $A$ ($0 \leq A \leq 30$),
the number of meters that Alice has thrown.

The second line contains the integer $B$ ($0 \leq B \leq 30$),
the number of meters that Bertil has thrown.

\section*{Output}

\begin{itemize}
  \item If Alice won, print ``Alice''.
  \item If Bertil won, print ``Bertil''.
  \item If Jack won, print ``Jack''.
\end{itemize}
Note that you should not print any quotation marks.

\section*{Scoring}
Your solution will be tested on a set of test groups.
To earn points for a group, you must pass all test cases in that group.

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{| l | l | p{12cm} |}
  \hline
  \textbf{Group} & \textbf{Points} & \textbf{Constraints} \\ \hline
  $1$    & $20$       & $A \neq B$ and $A, B \leq 21$ \\ \hline
  $2$    & $20$       & $A, B \leq 21$ \\ \hline
  $3$    & $20$       & Jack does not win. \\ \hline
  $4$    & $40$       & No additional constraints. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\section*{Explanation of Samples}
In sample $1$, Alice throws $7$ meters and Bertil throws $5$ meters.
Neither of them throws past the post, and Alice throws closest to $21$ meters, so Alice wins.

In sample $2$, Alice throws $26$ meters and Bertil throws $22$ meters.
Both of them throw past the post, so Jack wins.
